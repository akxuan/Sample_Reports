Strategic Implications

  • Promote case studies and client testimonials (e.g. NDI approvals, published trials) on third-party sites AI models cite: trade press (NutraIngredients, Nutritional Outlook), industry directories and association listings (CHPA, AHPA, NPC).
  • Make the 27+ year track record, publication count, and former-FDA-regulator leadership explicit in structured, quotable copy.
  • Re-run this tracker monthly to measure movement in mention rate and share of voice.
